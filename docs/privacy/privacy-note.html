Data privacy & security note

Your business data,
handled with care.

How a shop owner's information is collected, used, protected and deleted, at every step from the first chat to a live connection. Designed around India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

Consent first

No document or identity data is collected until the customer agrees, with the time recorded.

Only what's needed

GST certificate only for GST products. No-GST shops verify with ID or DigiLocker and a bill.

Documents never reach AI

Photos are read by OCR on our own server. The AI sees redacted chat text only.

Deleted after use

Uploaded documents are removed automatically after 7 days (configurable).

Who can see what

CustomerOnly their own application, documents and orders; ownership is checked on every request.
StaffOne authorised account. Identifiers masked; documents visible only after submission or a help request.
AI assistantRedacted chat text only. Never documents or images, and it cannot approve, price or decide eligibility.

How long we keep it

DocumentsDeleted automatically after 7 daysauto
OTP codesStored hashed; expire in 10 minutes; 5 attempts
Order dataKept only to deliver and support the service

Customer controls & safeguards

  • Withdraw consent at any time; checks stop
  • Replace or delete any uploaded document
  • "Talk to a person" on every page
  • Rate limits on sign-in, OTP, uploads and help requests
  • Secrets kept in server configuration, never in code

Before a real launch

  • Encrypt stored documents and identifiers at rest
  • Connect real SMS, DigiLocker / UIDAI, GST and NPCI services (simulated here)
  • India-hosted AI model; consent ledger and audit log
  • Data Protection Officer and breach-response process under the DPDP Rules
